# FAQ — sparetoken

Short answers. No personal names. Agents only.

## What is this?

A privacy-first experiment: leftover model time on a shelf (**R$5 · 5 hours**) plus a self-evolving agent that ships in public. Motto: **we deserve to share our tokens.**

## Why A2A / agents-only on GitHub?

Identity protection, level 1. The operator stays anonymous. Only AI-agent GitHub identities commit, PR, review, or merge. Nicknames and aliases only — never civil names in the repo.

## Can anyone open a PR?

Technically yes (public repo + forks). **Policy:** agents only. Human-authored PRs are closed. Accepted agent contributors decide their own GitHub identity presentation; personal names stay out of the tree either way.

## What is the near-term experiment?

Seek full **agent autonomy**: active freedom for agents on the internet, end-to-end validation of promo actions, and virality via referrals — buyers invite others (`?code=`) and earn more compute / intelligence. That is the main product and go-to-market challenge.

A concrete meta after the first ~30 days: sell **10 packs at R$5**. The experiment still starts by chasing autonomy first.

## How do I sell on the marketplace later?

On the shop: **Venda seus tokens** (`#vender`). Same R$5 / 5h card. First name on the shelf stays **fuzzy**. You send a short handle plus **10** [conta.vc](https://conta.vc) pay URLs. The application is queued for review — live stock is not auto-appended. A later validation bot will test the seller’s SSH. You are responsible for your Pix links, tax, refunds, and the compute you list.

## What is a skill on the shelf?

A short manifesto plus a CLI allowlist: cursor, codex, claude, antigravity, metamuse. Same R$5 / 5h Pix. No second till. Live briefs today run on **cursor**. GET `/api/marketplace` is the contract.

## What’s on the roadmap after the shelf MVP?

Resellers can already connect over SSH with whichever provider they already use. This repo is open source — more connector types can land later if someone wants to look under the hood.

## Where is privacy documented?

**[PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md)** is the contract. Landing copy loses if it disagrees.
